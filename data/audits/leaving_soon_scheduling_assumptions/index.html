Leaving Soon scheduling assumptions

A visual review of historical Seattle AMC schedules. The charts describe what the ledger shows. They do not change the production Leaving Soon model.

Observation window 2026-06-29 → 2026-10-01. As-of 2026-10-01. Base commit 73a858df4b610bc1ced5c54c26cf6caa16e27634.

Snapshots: 95 days, 29 complete, 66 unknown (records present, restate_safe missing). Missing calendar days: 0. First complete snapshot: 2026-09-03.

Film×theater engagements 1372. Confirmed film×theater ends 956. Confirmed market ends 290. Catalog rerelease engagements 81. Catalog special engagements 467.

How to read this

  • A confirmed end means the run has no later scheduled showtime and its last occurred show is strictly before that theater's dense publication horizon. Runs still on the schedule, and runs that stop where the published calendar stops, stay censored.
  • Film×theater is one booking at one AMC. Market-level is the last Seattle AMC showtime for that source film id, across theaters.
  • Catalog presentation.category identifies confident rereleases and specials. Title patterns that disagree with a standard catalog category are in the ambiguous tables, not in the primary percentages.
  • Charts that line runs up by their eventual final date, or that compare an initial announcement with the eventual schedule, are retrospective. They use information that would not be known on a prediction date.

Prior work this audit does not replace

  • docs/leaving-soon-lifecycle-audit.md and reel_seattle/analysis/amc_run_lifecycle.py already define source-film run identity, 14-day dark-gap splits, and right-censoring for remaining-days labels.
  • docs/leaving-soon-model-design.md and reel_seattle/analysis/amc_booking_cycle.py measure which weekday a visible horizon extension is first observed. In that older window, Thursday was about 9.5–10.3% of extension observations. That is not the weekday of a run's final showtime.
  • docs/leaving-soon-survival-model-v1.md reports segment performance. Rereleases were the weaker slice. It does not reconstruct whether an engagement was published in full on day one.

1. What day of the week do theatrical runs actually end?

Observed figures

  • Observed: 44.0% of confirmed ordinary film×theater runs with span ≥ 7 days end on Thursday (n=336). Wilson interval 38.8%–49.4%.
  • Observed Wednesday share in that same population: 53.6% (180 runs). Friday / Saturday / Sunday: 0.0% / 0.0% / 0.0%.
  • Observed theater Thursday shares differ. See end_weekday_thursday_rate_by_theater.png. The non-Thursday remainder at the low-Thursday theaters is mostly Wednesday.
  • Observed market-level ordinary week-plus Thursday share: 52.3% (n=86).
  • Observed catalog-rerelease Thursday share: 15.9% (n=63). Catalog specials: 15.3% (n=287).
  • Among publication-frontier cases, n=0. The dense horizon in this window sits after as-of, so historical endings are not censored for an unpublished next week. Still-scheduled runs, weekday of the farthest booked date: Thursday 32.5% (n=80).
  • Of the confirmed ordinary week-plus exits, 100.0% have the next programming Friday already published at the theater (n=336 of 336).
  • The 8 Tuesday endings in the ordinary week-plus set concentrate on 2026-09-08 (8 of them). That date is the day after Labor Day 2026.

Caveats

  • Snapshots are once a day. “+1 day” and “48 hours” mean the next daily snapshot or the snapshot two days later, not an exact hour.
  • A lag counts only when that calendar day’s snapshot exists and is on or before as-of. A missing day is left out of the denominator. It is not treated as a removal or as an empty schedule.
  • Screenings removed later are out of the eventual denominator, matching the original share. A removal is counted on a daily row only when that snapshot is complete.
  • “By 7 days before opening” and “by 3 days before opening” include only engagements we were already observing that early. The sample is smaller on purpose.
  • Batman (1989) is catalog category standard. It is in the focus tables because it was named for inspection. It is not in the catalog-rerelease percentages unless its segment is rerelease.
  • An unconfirmed end status means the ledger’s last date may still move. Those rows are in the daily CSV and are outside the rate tables.

Caveats

  • The denominator is closed: Friday screenings that were not removed and whose date is already before as-of. A future Friday can still gain screenings, so those dates are left out.

Data-quality notes

  • Daily logs run 2026-06-29 through 2026-10-01 with 0 missing dates. Complete-snapshot removals start 2026-09-03. Before that, absence is not treated as a cancellation.
  • Occurred means the show date is strictly before as-of (2026-10-01). The as-of snapshot is an early-morning scrape, so that calendar day is still future.
  • Film identity prefers AMC source_film_id. Title-only rows are a separate low-confidence segment and are not merged into a numeric movie id.
  • Enabled Seattle-area AMC theaters only. A film leaving one theater can continue at another; those are different rows.
  • no_longer_observed is inferred only on complete snapshots. Earlier unknown snapshots still record showtimes that were present.
