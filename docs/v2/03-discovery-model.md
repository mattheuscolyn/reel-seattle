**Status:** Seeded placeholders — behavior not defined

Discovery is central to Reel Seattle v2: users should encounter worthwhile opportunities **before** they disappear. This document records agreed concept names as placeholders only.

**Do not treat anything below as UX, ranking, or product behavior.** Definitions will be authored incrementally by Product Owner + ChatGPT.

---

## Agreed framing (non-behavioral)

* Discovery comes **before** reference use.
* Opportunities drive decisions; films summarize and group them.
* Inline exploration is preferred over unnecessary navigation.
