# 01 — Product Philosophy

**Status:** Seeded from agreed product direction  
**Related:** [README](./README.md) · [Core concepts](./02-core-concepts.md) · [Discovery model](./03-discovery-model.md) · [Opportunity model](./10-opportunity-model.md)

This document records the product principles already agreed for Reel Seattle v2. It is not an implementation guide.

---

## Mission

Reel Seattle helps people engage with cinema more deeply by making moviegoing opportunities visible before they disappear.

---

## Product Promise

Users should feel confident that if something extraordinary is happening in Seattle cinema, Reel Seattle will make sure they know about it in time to act.

---

## Emotional Goal

One year after launch, users should feel that Reel Seattle eliminated moviegoing regret. They no longer discover missed screenings after the fact—they confidently know which opportunities were worth considering, even if they ultimately chose not to attend.

---

## Core Product Model

```text
Film
  ↓
Opportunity
  ↓
Plan
```

* **Film** provides identity.
* **Opportunity** represents a specific way to experience that film.
* **Plan** represents commitment.

Do not expand this model into schemas, APIs, or UI layouts in this document. See [Core concepts](./02-core-concepts.md) for the high-level definitions.

---

## Product Principles

* Mobile-first.
* Information-dense.
* Highly interactive.
* Discovery before reference.
* Group by film.
* Opportunities drive decisions.
* Opportunities are summarized through films.
* Users should rarely need to leave Reel Seattle to answer cinema-related questions.
* Inline exploration is preferred over unnecessary navigation.
* The production site remains stable while v2 evolves independently.
